7. Your rights

Under the NDPA you may ask us to give you access to your personal data, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to how we use it, or provide it in a portable form. Where we rely on your consent, you may withdraw it at any time.

To exercise any of these, email asarpaul8@gmail.com. We respond within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

8. Security

We apply appropriate technical and organisational measures, keep access to personal data limited to those who need it, and will notify you and the Commission of a breach where the NDPA requires it.
