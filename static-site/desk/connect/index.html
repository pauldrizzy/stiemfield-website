Convergence Desk

Connect your email

This connects the desk to your Gmail, so reports, follow-ups and proposals go out by themselves. Your App Password is encrypted on this device before it is sent, and only the firm's server can read it.

  1. Turn on 2-Step Verification for your Google account (skip if it is already on).
  2. Create an App Password named "Stiemfield website" and copy the 16 letters.
  3. Enter the setup code from your Telegram, then paste the App Password below.
