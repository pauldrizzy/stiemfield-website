The STIEM Framework

Five forces govern every organisation. They are multiplicative — a near-zero force nullifies the rest. Our Convergence Map scores each force twice: its strength on its own terms, and how well it connects to the other four. Every score carries an Evidence Reliability rating.

SStrategy — does it govern real decisions, or decorate a shelf?
TTechnology — deployed, or actually integrated into management?
IInnovation — do pilots ever scale into operations?
EExecution — the measurable gap between decision and delivery
MManagement — aligned leadership that builds lasting capacity
